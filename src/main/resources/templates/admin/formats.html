CSV formats for adding or updating people

Save from Excel as CSV UTF-8. The first row must be the header exactly as shown. Upload it under People → Upload a CSV file. The files you download from the portal use the same columns, so you can edit a download and upload it back.

How rows are treated
  • The e-mail identifies the person. A new e-mail creates an account; an existing e-mail updates that person's details.
  • Everyone signs in with their college Google account (the e-mail in the file), so no passwords are involved. Students add their phone number at first login.
  • A row with a problem is skipped and listed after the upload; the other rows still import.
  • Empty optional cells leave the existing value unchanged.
