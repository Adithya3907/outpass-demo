Outpass Management System - live demo

Pick any role below and use the system exactly as that person would. Everything here is fictional sample data; nothing is real and nothing is sent to anyone. The demo resets by itself every few minutes.

Try the whole flow in 2 minutes

  1. Enter as Student → New outpass request (choose a reason, keep today, send to My Tutors).
  2. Click Switch role (yellow bar at the top) → enter as Tutor → open the request → press Approve.
  3. Switch back to Student → open the request: your QR code with a live countdown appears.
  4. Switch to Security guard → the gate page lists approved passes → press Scan → see the green VERIFIED screen.
  5. Look around as HOD, Principal and Admin to see reports, downloads and management screens.
